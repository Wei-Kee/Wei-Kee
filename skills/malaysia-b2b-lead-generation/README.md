# Malaysia B2B Lead Generation

A personal Codex skill for LOW WEI KEE covering Malaysian company research, official contact verification, qualification, partnership emails and follow-up tracking.

## Install

Copy this folder to `~/.codex/skills/malaysia-b2b-lead-generation`, or ask Codex's skill installer to install from:

https://github.com/Wei-Kee/Wei-Kee/tree/main/skills/malaysia-b2b-lead-generation

The skill becomes available on the next turn. Invoke `$malaysia-b2b-lead-generation` or describe a matching task.

## Usage

Ask it to research potential partners, cite official contact sources, qualify fit, draft emails and maintain a tracker. Connect Gmail or Outlook separately for mailbox access.

## Personalization and approval

This skill is configured for LOW WEI KEE. Other users should replace the sender identity before use. It must never invent a company, customer history or results. Every outgoing email, including replies and follow-ups, requires explicit approval of the exact message and recipients.

## Data boundary

This public package contains instructions only. Keep credentials, private email correspondence and live customer records outside this repository. Officially published contact details do not prove deliverability or consent.
